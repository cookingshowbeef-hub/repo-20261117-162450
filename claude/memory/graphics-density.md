The user rejected a Pagpag cut (Nov 2026) for being "mostly voice over on footage and image, barely any motion graphic that add to the script", comparing it with the Aral Sea V2 film, which carries about 45 on-screen graphics in 12 minutes: stat callouts, keyword slams, name tags, part title cards and data visuals.

**Why:** Netflix-doc craft for this channel means graphics carry information (numbers, definitions, lists, timelines, process steps), and a cut with only around 8 cards feels unedited.

**How to apply:** Plan a graphics pass alongside the edit, aiming for at least 3 to 4 per minute, each pinned to the spoken words. Use act title cards, stats, word lists, timelines, diagrams and quotes, with legible scrims. Pagpag's version is `overlay_spec.py`, then `overlay_pass.py`, then `render_ov.mjs`, then `apply_overlays.py`. See [[documentary-style-direction]] and [[music-and-motion-tool-choices]].
